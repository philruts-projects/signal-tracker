# Stakeholder Brief — Companies House Signal Tracker

**Commissioned by:** Head of Commercial Risk (owns credit, key-account and supplier-risk exposure)
**Prepared by:** Data & AI Enablement · **Date:** August 2026 · **Status:** Approved, in build

## The problem

Three teams each depend on knowing when a UK company they care about changes — and all three find out late, by accident, or not at all. Credit needs early warning of distress before extending or renewing a line. Account management needs to know when a key account restructures or changes ownership. Procurement needs to spot a critical supplier filing signs of trouble.

Every one of these signals is already public and free. Companies House publishes them the day they are filed. The problem is not access to the data — it is that nobody is watching it systematically. Analysts check a handful of companies manually, ad hoc, usually only after something has already gone wrong.

## What we want

A tool that continuously monitors a defined watchlist of UK companies via the free Companies House API, detects significant filing events, and produces a short, plain-English commercial briefing for each one — what happened, what it likely means, and what action the relevant team should consider — generated by the Claude API so a non-specialist reads a clear paragraph, not a filing code.

## Scope

**In scope:** a configurable watchlist; automated detection of new filings (accounts, confirmation statements, charges, director changes, insolvency and Gazette notices); AI-generated briefings with a suggested action; a lightweight UI; an evaluation of accuracy against known events.

**Out of scope (this version):** credit scoring or a formal risk model; non-UK entities; automated actions (the tool informs a human, it does not decide); CRM or ledger integration.

## What success looks like

- **Coverage** — detects a materially higher share of significant filing events than manual checking does today.
- **Timeliness** — a relevant event surfaces within one polling cycle of being filed, not weeks later.
- **Briefing quality** — on a test set of known events, the summary is accurate and the suggested action sensible, with failure modes documented honestly.
- **Usability** — a non-technical user can add a company and understand a briefing with no training.

## Constraints

Free Companies House API. Briefings are advisory only and always cite the underlying filing. Single-user, portfolio-grade prototype — reliability, auth and multi-user concerns explicitly deferred.
